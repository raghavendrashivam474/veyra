# S1 — Candidate Trait Taxonomy (Experimental)

**Status:** Experimental — subject to change based on evidence
**Created:** 2026-09-13

> **WARNING:** This is NOT a frozen domain ontology.
> Traits may be added, removed, renamed, split, merged, or
> reclassified based on S1 experimental results.

---

## Taxonomy

### identity/ (structural features)
- identity.facial_structure — overall face shape (oval, round, angular, etc.)
- identity.eye_structure — eye shape and set (almond, round, wide-set, etc.)
- identity.nose_structure — nose shape (narrow, wide, button, etc.)
- identity.mouth_structure — mouth/lip shape

### appearance/ (surface features)
- ppearance.hair_color — dominant hair color
- ppearance.hair_length — hair length category
- ppearance.hair_texture — hair texture (straight, wavy, curly, etc.)
- ppearance.skin_appearance — skin tone/appearance descriptor

### body/ (proportional features)
- ody.build — overall body build
- ody.apparent_height — relative height impression
- ody.proportions — notable proportional characteristics

### clothing/ (garment features)
- clothing.garment_type — primary clothing items
- clothing.dominant_colors — dominant clothing colors
- clothing.accessories — visible accessories

### style/ (aesthetic features)
- style.rendering_style — art style (photorealistic, anime, painterly, etc.)
- style.visual_aesthetic — overall aesthetic impression

### state/ (transient features)
- state.pose — body/head pose
- state.expression — facial expression

---

## Notes

- identity/* traits are hypothesized to be most stable and
  identity-relevant. This is a hypothesis to test, not a conclusion.
- state/* traits are hypothesized to be least useful for persistent
  identity. Also to be tested.
- clothing/* and style/* may be useful for character "look" but
  weak for identity. Evidence needed.
